Logins

You start logged in as the administrator. To try another role, log out (top right of the dashboard), then log in with one of these.

LoginPasswordCan do
volunteercouncil8600Edit pages, events and photos
faithcouncil8600Program owner: update only the Faith page and their own events
corcouncil8600Program owner: update only the COR Mass page and their own events

The members-only documents page uses the password members.

Two things to try as volunteer

  1. Add an event. Dashboard → Events → Add event. Type a title, fill in “When and where” on the right, then press Publish. It appears on the home page and the Events page.
  2. Swap the Knight of the Month photo. Pages → Recognition → click the photo → Replace → pick a photo → change the name → Save.

Prototype only: names, dates and phone numbers are placeholders, and forms send nothing. The official site is www.kofc8600.org.
